## The context

SIMS is the business application of Les Menus Services. It is being redesigned: some features have already moved to the new interface, the rest is still on the old one. My work covers the food offer scope.

## The approach

- understand the current user journeys and their pain points;
- propose new screens in **Figma**, following the application's design system;
- build prototype templates to show the interactions, starting with popups;
- present the proposals to the team and iterate.

## What I take away

Working on an application that people use every day means improving without breaking anything: each screen has to stay consistent with what users already know.
