## The goal

Analyse a ransomware sample end to end and produce deliverables a security team can use.

## The five steps

1. identifying the sample;
2. analysing its behaviour;
3. understanding the encryption mechanism;
4. recovering the files;
5. writing the report.

## The deliverables

- a **14-page report**;
- a **recovery tool** written and commented in Python;
- a list of **indicators of compromise** (IOC);
- an architecture diagram.
