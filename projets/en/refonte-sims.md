## The context

Alongside the mockups, I work on the infrastructure side of the SIMS redesign at Les Menus Services.

## What I did

- **containerised** the applications with Docker;
- set up a **CI/CD pipeline** to build, test and deploy automatically;
- **monitoring** with Prometheus and Grafana dashboards to track the health of the services.

## What I take away

Working on both the infrastructure and the interface of the same product gives me the full picture: I know how a screen will be served, and who it is designed for.
