## The need

At Les Menus Services, several application modules are developed in parallel. The team needed a simple way to see where each module stands.

## What I built

A dark-themed web application with:

- side navigation;
- a **Kanban** board to track the status of each module;
- key indicators at the top of the page;
- creation and deletion of modules and sub-screens;
- local saving in the browser.
